Known professionally as OWN GEZI, Magezi Norah is a Ugandan singer, songwriter, vocal trainer and creative artist whose work brings together musical expression, storytelling and the human voice.

Raised in Uganda, she grew up with an early connection to music through her father, Herbert Bugembe, a music lecturer and educator whose work introduced her to the world of musical learning and expression.

After losing her father during childhood, her connection with music continued to grow through choir participation, singing and storytelling. During her school years, she took on choir leadership and continued developing her voice and musical understanding.

Her journey has since grown to include songwriting, vocal mentorship and contributions to film soundtracks. In 2026, she began her professional music career under Renaissance Studios.
